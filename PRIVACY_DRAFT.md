## What we collect

**Quote form** (Get a Quote): your name, email address and phone number (optional); your
event type, date, start and end time and guest count; the venue name, venue type and venue
address; the setups and quantities you choose; any custom branding notes and special
requests you type in.

**Contact form**: your name, surname (optional), email address, phone number (optional) and
your message.

We only receive what you type into these forms. If you phone, WhatsApp, email or message us
on Instagram instead, we receive whatever you send us that way.

## Why we use it

- to reply to your message or quote request;
- to prepare and send your quote, and to plan and deliver your event if you book;
- to keep a record of your enquiry and booking.

When you send a form, our team receives an email with your details, and you receive an
email confirming we got it. If you book, we send you a reminder email before your event. We
may contact you by email, phone or WhatsApp about your enquiry or event. We do not add you
to a marketing mailing list from these forms, and we do not sell your information.

## Where it goes

The forms send your details over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our own booking system,
which stores them in our database and sends the emails described above through our email
service. Only our team can access it.
